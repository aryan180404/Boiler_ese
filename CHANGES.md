# Website change log

Changes from the first version, which showed one forecast (1 minute ahead) from 35 inputs.

## Data (`data.js`)
- Regenerated by section 15 of `Boiler_steam_temperature.ipynb`. Rerun that notebook to rebuild it.
- 400 random moments from the test period. Each has all 35 inputs, the outlet temperature for the 10 minutes before and after (every 30 seconds), and, for each horizon, the actual value, the selected model's forecast and the linear regression forecast.
- Each horizon lists the inputs it uses: 21 at 1 minute, 35 at 5 and 10 minutes.

## Models shown
| Horizon | Model used | Mean absolute error | Linear regression alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 minute | Linear regression + tuned Random Forest correction | 0.134 °C | 0.139 °C |
| 5 minutes | Equal-weight average of linear regression and tuned XGBoost | 0.957 °C | 1.061 °C |
| 10 minutes | Equal-weight average of linear regression and tuned LightGBM | 2.182 °C | 2.217 °C |

## Page (`index.html`, `app.js`, `styles.css`)
The page keeps the original dashboard design, with these changes:
- **Introduction** now describes the 1, 5 and 10 minute forecasts and the 21 inputs.
- **1 min / 5 min / 10 min toggle** beside "Show another test row". It switches the large forecast, the actual value, the error, the chart and the boiler readout.
- **Status line** keeps the original wording, for example "Larger than the model's mean error of 0.134 °C." The value is the mean error of the selected horizon's model.
- **Model line** under the status names the model used, such as "Linear regression with a tuned Random Forest correction (hybrid)".
- **Chart** shows all three forecasts at once: the measured temperature over the 10 minutes before the test row (lighter after "Now", showing what was measured next), the forecasts at +1, +5 and +10 minutes with their mean error bands, and the actual values. The selected horizon is drawn darker. "Measured" is a single legend entry, since both lines are measured temperatures.
- **New table, "This moment at every horizon"**: for the current test row it shows the actual and predicted value at 1, 5 and 10 minutes, the model's mean absolute error, and how much lower that error is than linear regression alone. Clicking a row switches to that horizon.
- **Inputs panel** lists the inputs the selected horizon uses (21 at 1 minute, 35 at 5 and 10). The footnote gives that horizon's scores and explains which sensors it uses.
- **Boiler drawing, animation, colours and fonts** are unchanged.

## Small-screen and units fixes
- **"This moment at every horizon"** becomes one card per horizon on screens narrower than 720 pixels, so no column is cut off. Wider screens keep the table.
- **Inputs panel** shows °C beside every temperature. Other sensors have no unit because the data set does not record one; add `"units": { "sensor name": "unit" }` to `data.js` and the page will show them.
- **Boiler drawing** on small screens has a soft right edge to show that it scrolls, and can be scrolled from the keyboard.
